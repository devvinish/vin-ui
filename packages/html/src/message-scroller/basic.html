I'm going to Lisbon in March. Any tips?

March is lovely there: mild, and fewer crowds than summer. Bring good shoes, the hills are steep.

Where should I stay?

Alfama for old streets and views, Chiado for shops and cafés, or Príncipe Real if you want somewhere quieter.

How do I get from the airport?

The metro's red line goes straight to the city in about 25 minutes. A taxi takes 15 to 20 minutes.

Is a day trip to Sintra worth it?

Yes. Take the train from Rossio, go early, and book Pena Palace tickets ahead.
